Looking ahead

The Future of Software Engineering

Across our different perspectives, a common theme emerges: AI is changing how software gets made, but engineering judgment still matters. We expect the work to shift toward understanding problems, designing systems, and making sound decisions as AI takes on more repetitive implementation. That shift also raises hard questions about trust, security, reliability, energy use, and how people adapt to new tools and roles.

Design over repetition

System architecture, requirements, and thoughtful decisions become central as routine coding is increasingly automated.

Trust is engineered

Generated work needs human understanding, testing, and verification to become a reliable product.

Change is not certainty

AI’s impact may be significant, but its limits—including energy demands—and how society adapts remain open questions.
