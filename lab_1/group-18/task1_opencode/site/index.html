Shared view

Our take on AI and the future of software engineering

A few things we think matter, even as the tools change.

We asked an AI to predict the future. It said “it depends”.

Agent: I did not break it. I refactored it.

First drafts are cheap; judgement is the bottleneck.

Generated code is a starting point. Engineers still need to decide whether it is correct, useful, and reliable.

Prompting is not engineering.

Understanding systems, architecture, security, and failure modes is how we use AI well and evaluate what it produces.

Humans stay accountable.

“The AI did it” is not an incident report. People remain responsible for the systems they ship.
