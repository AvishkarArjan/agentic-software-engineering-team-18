## What I think about AI in software engineering today
AI is changing the way software is developed, making coding faster and more accessible. However, I believe strong engineering fundamentals are becoming even more important. Understanding algorithms, system architecture, security, and problem-solving is essential to using AI effectively and evaluating the quality of its output.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years
Software engineers will increasingly move from writing every line of code themselves to designing systems, defining requirements, and coordinating AI-powered agents. Understanding complex problems, making sound technical decisions, and ensuring that systems are reliable and secure will become even more valuable.

## My hot take on AI
AI can generate code, but generating code is not the same as engineering a reliable product. I believe the real challenge is not just making AI more powerful, but making its output trustworthy, secure, efficient, and useful in real-world systems.
